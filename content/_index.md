# About me

I'm a computer science PhD student at UCLA, advised by Rafail Ostrovsky. My research focuses on cryptography and secure computation, particularly garbled circuits. Previously, I was a research engineer at Alpen Labs.

I'm also exploring AI safety and mechanistic interpretability, with a particular interest in how language models reason and use information from context.

## Interpretability project

### Investigating Attention Heads in In-Context Retrieval

This exploratory investigation builds on *Mixing Mechanisms*. I studied how changing the outputs of two attention heads in Qwen2.5-3B-Instruct affects retrieval of information supplied in a prompt.

- [Research write-up](/notes/investigating-attention-heads-in-in-context-retrieval/): experiments, results and limitations.
- [NNsight notebook](/notebooks/binding-heads/): self-contained code, saved GPU outputs and interactive attention visualizations.
- [Process note](/notes/in-context-retrieval-process/): why I chose the project, how I used AI and what I learned.
